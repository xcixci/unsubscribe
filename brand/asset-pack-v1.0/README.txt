UNSUBSCRIBE — BRAND ASSET PACK
Version 1.0 | 7 November 2026

Included
- Primary wordmark: dark, light and transparent PNG/SVG
- Strike-through campaign wordmark: dark and light PNG; SVG
- App icon: Ink, Paper and Alert variants
- Favicon PNGs: 16, 32, 64, 180, 192, 256 and 512 px
- 1024 px app icon master

Brand colours
Ink   #111111
Paper #F5F2EB
Alert #F04438
Accent #D7FF35

Usage
- Primary wordmark with red full stop is the default identity.
- Strike-through mark is for campaign/social use, not the default corporate mark.
- U-and-dot icon is the selected starting direction for app icon/favicon use.
- Lime is an accent colour; it is not used in the primary logo.

Production note
These are first-pass usable digital assets generated from the approved direction. The SVG files use live text with font fallbacks, not outlined glyph paths. For final commercial production, have a designer convert the approved lettering to vector outlines, refine kerning/geometry, test reproduction at small sizes, and confirm font licensing. These assets are not trademark clearance.
